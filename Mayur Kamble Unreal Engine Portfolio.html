Fire Extinguisher Training Simulation

Engine: Unreal Engine 5
Main VR Training Facility — Custom 3D layout with overhead fire suppression pipes, safety posters & seating

Project Overview

Designed and built entirely from scratch in Unreal Engine 5, this VR simulation program trains users on proper fire extinguisher operation (P.A.S.S. method) inside an industrial facility classroom environment.

The virtual room features custom-modeled overhead fire suppression pipes, safety chart wall mountings, office seating layout, glass partitions, and interactive fire safety equipment designed for realistic compliance training.

Technical Implementation

VR Hand Mechanics Grasp, pin pull, nozzle aim, and trigger squeeze via UE5 Controllers
Niagara VFX Particles Real-time foam/CO2 discharge and dynamic fire suppression
Environment Design Custom hard-surface piping, lighting setup, and classroom dressing
Blueprint Logic Step-by-step P.A.S.S. evaluation system and safety score tracking
